This first welcome note introduces the blog and summarises the workspace state it records.
It is kept as a draft because its editorial content has not yet been approved.

## Purpose

This site is where the Heretical workspace keeps its development-progress record: what
moved, why it moved, and what is still open. It is intentionally informal compared with
product documentation, and it never substitutes for a changelog, release note, or
architecture decision record.

## Current state

- The workspace composes first-party products, sites, and infrastructure deployments as
  individually versioned repositories, with the superproject pinning exact revisions.
- The documentation portal and this blog are separate website products with separate
  publication histories.
- No product of the engine, framework, or editor has been published yet, so early posts
  describe tooling and structure rather than shipped functionality.

## What to expect

Short notes after meaningful merged work, occasional decision write-ups, and curated
milestone or release posts when there is something to install. A merge is not a release,
and posts here will say which one happened.
